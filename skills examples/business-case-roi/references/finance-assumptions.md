# Finance Assumptions (Company Standard)

> Course note: replace these with your organisation's real numbers. This shows how a skill can carry company-specific knowledge.

## Discount rate
- Standard discount rate for all business cases: **8%** (`--discount-rate 0.08`)
- High-risk or unproven projects (new market, new technology): **12%**

## Analysis period
- Software / SaaS tools: **3 years**
- Hardware / equipment: **5 years**
- New hires: **3 years**

## Payback targets
- Software & automation: payback within **18 months**
- Hardware & equipment: payback within **36 months**

## Loaded hourly labour rates
Use these when converting time saved into dollars. They include salary, benefits, and overhead.

| Role level | Loaded hourly rate |
|---|---|
| Administrative / support | $40 |
| Individual contributor / analyst | $65 |
| Manager | $95 |
| Senior leader / director | $150 |

Working weeks per year: **46** (allowing for holidays and leave)

## Approval thresholds (based on upfront investment)
| Upfront investment | Approver |
|---|---|
| Under $10,000 | Department manager |
| $10,000 – $50,000 | Department director |
| $50,000 – $250,000 | VP + Finance |
| Over $250,000 | CFO + Executive committee |
